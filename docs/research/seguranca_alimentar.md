# Segurança Alimentar Research

## Overview
Analysis of food safety and traceability management systems relevant to the BusinessOS project, focusing on Brazilian regulatory compliance and supply chain transparency.

## Key Players Analysis

### SISAG (Sistema de Informação da Agricultura Familiar)
- **Core Functionality**: Government-mandated system for tracking food production, processing, and distribution. Ensures compliance with ANVISA (health regulatory agency) and MAPA (agriculture ministry) regulations.
- **Limitations**: Slow data entry process; limited real-time traceability features for complex supply chains.
- **Unique Value**: Direct integration with federal food safety inspection systems and emergency recall protocols.

### Infotrilha
- **Core Functionality**: Digital traceability platform for agricultural products from farm to table. Tracks ingredients, allergens, and expiration dates across supply chains.
- **Limitations**: Requires manual data entry for small producers; limited mobile functionality for field use.
- **Strength**: Blockchain-inspired audit trail for food safety certifications and recall management.

### Vigilância Sanitária Digital
- **Core Functionality**: Real-time food safety monitoring platform used by municipal health departments. Manages inspections, violation tracking, and corrective actions.
- **Limitations**: Fragmented data across different municipal systems; limited integration with private sector platforms.
- **Unique Value**: AI-powered risk scoring for food establishments based on historical violation data.

## Common Capabilities Across Solutions
- Digital record keeping for ingredients and allergens
- Compliance tracking for ANVISA, MAPA, and local health regulations
- Digital inspection management with photo documentation
- Basic traceability from production to consumption
- Mobile-responsive interfaces for health inspectors

## Critical Gaps Identified
1. **End-to-End Traceability**: Limited systems support full supply chain visibility from producer to consumer
2. **Real-Time Alerts**: Most systems lack immediate notification capabilities for food safety issues
3. **Small Producer Access**: Complex interfaces discourage small farm participation
4. **Interoperability Issues**: Incompatibility between government and private sector platforms

## Research Recommendations
1. Develop standardized traceability protocols compatible with all supply chain stages
2. Implement real-time alert systems for food safety incidents
3. Create simplified mobile interfaces for small producers
4. Establish public-private data sharing frameworks for comprehensive traceability
